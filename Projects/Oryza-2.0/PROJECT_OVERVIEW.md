# 🌾 Oryza Project Overview

## 📌 Use Case for Coding
• **Problem**: Traditional wealth management is expensive, inaccessible, and requires constant manual intervention
• **Target Users**: Retail investors, ESG-conscious investors, busy professionals, HNI/UHNI clients
• **Core Need**: Autonomous financial management that works 24/7 without human intervention
• **Key Scenarios**:
  - Automatic portfolio rebalancing based on market conditions
  - Proactive tax-loss harvesting to minimize tax liability
  - Real-time ESG compliance and values-based investing
  - Behavioral coaching to prevent emotional trading mistakes
  - Complete financial life management beyond just investing

## 💡 Solution & Benefits
• **Multi-Agent AI System**: 6 specialized AI agents negotiate optimal portfolios
• **Autonomous Trading**: Executes trades 24/7 based on market opportunities
• **Wealth Concierge**: Manages entire financial life - bills, taxes, savings
• **ESG Integration**: Real-time monitoring and automatic divestment from controversies
• **Key Benefits**:
  - Save 15-20 hours/month on financial management
  - Reduce investment costs by 60-80% vs traditional advisors
  - Improve returns through emotion-free, data-driven decisions
  - Achieve values alignment without sacrificing returns
  - Access institutional-grade strategies at retail prices

## 🚀 Innovativeness
• **First-of-its-kind**: Multi-agent negotiation for portfolio optimization
• **Game Theory**: Agents use Nash equilibrium for consensus building
• **Behavioral AI**: Predicts and prevents poor financial decisions
• **Autonomous Actions**: Goes beyond advice to actual execution
• **Technical Innovation**:
  - Swarm intelligence for emergent investment strategies
  - Real-time sentiment analysis affecting portfolio decisions
  - Satellite imagery for ESG verification
  - Voice UI and AR portfolio visualization
  - Federated learning for privacy-preserving personalization

## 👤 User Experience
• **Zero-effort Wealth Building**: Set preferences once, AI handles everything
• **Conversational Interface**: Talk to your wealth manager naturally
• **Real-time Transparency**: See exactly what AI is doing and why
• **Emotional Support**: Behavioral nudges during market volatility
• **Progressive Disclosure**: Simple for beginners, powerful for pros
• **Key UX Features**:
  - One-click emergency override
  - Explainable AI with SHAP/LIME visualizations
  - Gamified learning and achievements
  - Social trading and strategy sharing
  - Mobile-first responsive design
