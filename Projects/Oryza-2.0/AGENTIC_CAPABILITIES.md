## 🤖 Multi-Agent Autonomous System

### 1. **Autonomous Trading Agent**
- **Continuous Market Surveillance**: Scans 500+ stocks every minute
- **Consensus-Based Execution**: Multiple specialized agents must agree (>80% confidence) before trading
- **Auto-funding**: Directly debits linked bank accounts for approved trades
- **Position Management**: Monitors and adjusts stop-loss/take-profit levels in real-time
- **Learning Loop**: Improves strategies based on trade outcomes using reinforcement learning

### 2. **Equity Analysis Agent**
- **Earnings Call Monitoring**: Listens to live earnings calls and trades on sentiment
- **Technical Pattern Recognition**: Identifies and acts on chart patterns automatically
- **Fundamental Screening**: Continuously evaluates P/E, growth metrics, and valuations
- **Sector Rotation**: Automatically shifts allocations based on sector momentum

### 3. **Sentiment Analysis Agent** 
- **News Impact Trading**: Executes trades within seconds of breaking news
- **Social Media Monitoring**: Tracks Reddit, Twitter for retail sentiment shifts
- **Analyst Report Processing**: Parses and acts on analyst upgrades/downgrades
- **Controversy Detection**: Immediately exits positions on negative news

### 4. **Risk Management Agent**
- **Dynamic Position Sizing**: Adjusts position sizes based on market volatility
- **Correlation Management**: Prevents over-concentration in correlated assets
- **Drawdown Protection**: Automatically reduces exposure during market stress
- **Black Swan Hedging**: Maintains protective positions for tail events

### 5. **Portfolio Optimization Agent**
- **Continuous Rebalancing**: Maintains optimal asset allocation 24/7
- **Tax-Loss Harvesting**: Automatically sells losers and buys similar assets
- **Dividend Capture**: Executes trades to maximize dividend income
- **Factor Tilting**: Adjusts factor exposures based on market regimes

### 6. **ESG Compliance Agent**
- **Values Enforcement**: Automatically divests from companies violating user values
- **Impact Maximization**: Seeks highest ESG impact within return constraints
- **Greenwashing Detection**: Verifies ESG claims against multiple data sources
- **Controversy Response**: Immediate action on ESG-related controversies

### 7. **Wealth Concierge Agent**
- **Bill Negotiation**: Calls service providers to negotiate better rates
- **Subscription Optimization**: Cancels unused subscriptions automatically
- **Cash Flow Management**: Moves money between accounts for optimal interest
- **Spending Analysis**: Identifies and alerts on unusual spending patterns

### 8. **Goal Planning Agent**
- **Life Event Detection**: Adjusts strategies for detected life changes
- **Milestone Tracking**: Automatically increases savings for approaching goals
- **Goal Feasibility**: Re-calculates and adjusts goals based on market conditions
- **Alternative Pathways**: Suggests and implements alternative strategies
