# Salary, Standard Deduction and House Rent Allowance
> source: Income-tax Act, 1961 | rules: SALARY-INCOME, SALARY-STD-DED, HRA

## Income under the head Salaries
Salary, wages, allowances, perquisites and pension are taxed under the head
Salaries (Sections 15 to 17). Your employer reports the gross salary and the tax
deducted at source (TDS) on Form 16. The gross salary, reduced by the deductions
allowed under the head, gives the income chargeable under Salaries.

## Standard deduction (Section 16(ia))
A salaried individual (or pensioner) may claim a flat standard deduction with no
proof required. For FY 2024-25 (AY 2025-26) the standard deduction is Rs 75,000
under the new tax regime and Rs 50,000 under the old tax regime. It is subtracted
from the gross salary before any other computation.

## House Rent Allowance (Section 10(13A))
An employee who receives HRA and actually pays rent may claim an exemption, but
only under the old regime. The exempt amount is the least of: the actual HRA
received; rent paid minus 10% of salary; and 50% of salary in a metro city (40%
elsewhere). Because it depends on the salary break-up and the rent actually paid,
an HRA claim from a rent receipt should be confirmed with the taxpayer before it
is allowed.
