# Tax Slabs, Rebate, Surcharge and Cess (FY 2024-25)
> source: Finance (No. 2) Act, 2024 | rules: SLAB-NEW-2024, SLAB-OLD-2024, REBATE-87A, SURCHARGE, CESS

## New tax regime slabs (Section 115BAC) for FY 2024-25
The new regime is the default. For FY 2024-25 (AY 2025-26) the slabs, the same for
every age, are:

- Up to Rs 3,00,000: nil
- Rs 3,00,001 to Rs 7,00,000: 5%
- Rs 7,00,001 to Rs 10,00,000: 10%
- Rs 10,00,001 to Rs 12,00,000: 15%
- Rs 12,00,001 to Rs 15,00,000: 20%
- Above Rs 15,00,000: 30%

Only the standard deduction and the employer's NPS contribution are allowed; most
Chapter VI-A deductions are not.

## Old tax regime slabs for FY 2024-25
The old regime keeps the Chapter VI-A deductions but uses higher rates. The basic
exemption limit rises with age:

- Below 60: nil up to Rs 2,50,000, then 5% to Rs 5,00,000, 20% to Rs 10,00,000, 30% above.
- Senior citizen (60 to under 80): nil up to Rs 3,00,000.
- Super senior (80 and above): nil up to Rs 5,00,000.

## Rebate under Section 87A
A resident individual pays no tax if total income is within the rebate threshold.
Under the new regime the rebate is up to Rs 25,000 when total income does not
exceed Rs 7,00,000 (with marginal relief just above it). Under the old regime the
rebate is up to Rs 12,500 when total income does not exceed Rs 5,00,000.

## Surcharge and Health & Education Cess
A surcharge applies to the income tax when total income exceeds Rs 50,00,000: 10%
above Rs 50 lakh, 15% above Rs 1 crore, 25% above Rs 2 crore, and 37% above Rs 5
crore (the new regime caps the top rate at 25%). Marginal relief limits the
surcharge near each threshold. Finally, a Health & Education Cess of 4% is added to
the income tax plus surcharge.
