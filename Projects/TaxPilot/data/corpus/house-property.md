# Home-Loan Interest on a Self-Occupied House (Section 24(b))
> source: Income-tax Act, 1961 | rules: SEC24B

Interest paid on a loan borrowed to buy, build, repair or reconstruct a house is
deductible under the head Income from House Property. For a self-occupied
property the deduction for interest is capped at Rs 2,00,000 per year, and this
creates a loss under the head that can be set off against other income.

The set-off is allowed under the old regime only; the new regime does not permit
the self-occupied home-loan interest deduction. The lender issues an interest
certificate showing the interest component of the year's repayments, which is the
figure claimed (up to the Rs 2,00,000 ceiling).
