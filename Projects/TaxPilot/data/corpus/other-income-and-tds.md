# Income from Other Sources and Tax Deducted at Source
> source: Income-tax Act, 1961 | rules: INTEREST-INCOME, TDS

## Income from other sources (Section 56)
Interest from bank deposits, fixed deposits and bonds, and most other residual
income, is taxed under the head Income from Other Sources. Bank interest is
reported on an interest certificate and is taxable in full (a savings-account slice
may separately qualify for the Section 80TTA/80TTB deduction under the old regime).

## Tax deducted at source (Sections 192 and 194)
Tax is often collected in advance by deduction at source: an employer deducts TDS
on salary under Section 192 and reports it on Form 16, while banks and other
payers deduct TDS under Section 194-series provisions and report it on Form 16A.
All of it is consolidated in Form 26AS (and the Annual Information Statement).

TDS is a prepayment of your tax. It is subtracted from the total tax liability to
arrive at the refund due or the balance still payable. The TDS claimed on the
return should match Form 26AS; a claim in excess of Form 26AS is a common reason a
refund is held up.
