    How it works

    1. Guardrails screen the documents (injection, PAN/Aadhaar inventory).
    2. Fields are extracted from Form 16, 26AS and proofs; income is normalized.
    3. Deductions are grounded in retrieved Income-tax Act sections.
    4. A deterministic engine computes both regimes and keeps the cheaper.
    5. Audit risk is scored; uncertain returns go to review.
    6. Every rupee figure in the report is checked against the engine.
